# DueOne — Product Concept

## What is DueOne?

DueOne is a private, intimate space for a small group of 2 to 5 people.
It is not a general social platform. It is designed for close relationships where members want to share a schedule, have private conversations, and stay connected — without the noise of larger apps.

A group in DueOne is called a **Space**. Each Space has members, a shared calendar, and a secret chat room.

Typical use cases:
- A couple tracking anniversaries, dates, and private messages
- A group of 3–4 close friends planning trips or events
- A family pod keeping shared schedules
- A small study group coordinating sessions and exams

---

## Why 2 to 5 people?

The size limit is intentional and is core to the product's identity.

**Small groups communicate differently.** When a group is small, every message is visible to everyone and each person matters. There is no audience, no performance — only real connection.

**Scaling breaks intimacy.** Once a group exceeds 5 people, dynamics shift: some members go silent, subgroups form, and the shared space starts to feel like a public channel rather than a private one. DueOne is designed to stay small on purpose.

**Feature design follows group size.** The calendar, chat, and member features are all designed for the assumption that every member knows every other member personally. There are no roles beyond owner and member, no follower model, and no public discovery.

---

## How is DueOne different?

### vs. KakaoTalk / WhatsApp / iMessage
These are messaging platforms. They support thousands of contacts and group chats of any size. They do not have shared calendars, space concepts, or structured relationship types. DueOne adds structure (calendar, space identity) and limits size intentionally.

### vs. Google Calendar
Google Calendar is a personal calendar with optional sharing. It is not designed around relationships. You can share calendars, but there is no "space", no chat, no member identity, and no concept of a close group. DueOne treats the calendar as one feature within a shared relational space.

### vs. Notion
Notion is a productivity and knowledge tool. It is flexible but general-purpose. It does not provide a real-time feel, a chat room, or a relationship-first structure. DueOne is not a workspace — it is a personal space.
